India Today:

A FlyDubai flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv landed safely at Saudi Arabia’s Tabuk airport on Wednesday after declaring an emergency, following a dramatic mid-air incident that briefly raised fears of a hijacking and prompted Israel to scramble fighter jets. Flight FZ1073, carrying around 180 people, was flying towards Ben Gurion Airport when it transmitted the international emergency squawk code 7700 and later the 7500 code, which indicates hijacking or unlawful interference. The aircraft was diverted from its route and eventually landed at Tabuk, where Saudi security forces surrounded the plane.

Banglore Today:

Karnatakada Nudi Thota, a literary and cultural festival, will be held at Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, Race Course Road, from October 2 to October 4.The event will mark the 60th anniversary of the Bengaluru chapter of Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan and celebrate lesser-known regional languages across Karnataka.“We will celebrate indigenous languages like Sankethi, Tulu, Konkani, Beary, Arebhashe, and many others,” says H N Suresh, the director of Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, Bengaluru. Over three days, cultural programmes and talks led by scholars will spotlight languages that may be nearing “extinction”. “These talks will explore the past, present, and future of indigenous languages and will be preceded by a 15- to 20-minute performance,” says the centre’s joint director Nagalakshmi K Rao.

President Droupadi Murmu has congratulated Neeru Dhanda for winning India’s first individual Gold Medal at the Asian Games 2026.
